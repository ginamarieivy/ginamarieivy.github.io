About

Ginamarie
Ivy

I'm a Senior Graphic Designer and Art Director who builds brand systems that work everywhere — from billboards and event installations to email campaigns and social feeds. I specialize in translating complex, multi-channel briefs into cohesive visual worlds that feel both strategically sound and distinctly human.

Most recently at Indeed, I led design on campaigns reaching millions of job seekers across the country — including a Cannes Lions-recognized activation — and built cross-channel design systems that scaled across paid, organic, email, print, and events simultaneously.

Background

Designer, art director,
and occasional creative chaos agent

My work spans brand identity, campaign design, editorial, illustration, event environments, and photoshoot art direction. I'm equally comfortable building a design system from scratch and adapting it across eight co-branded partners, or rolling up my sleeves to art direct a full-day photo shoot.

I love work that has to solve a real problem — whether that's helping a nurse in Detroit discover a better job, making a job seeker in LA feel seen by a brand, or turning a sister-in-law's living room into a Costco warehouse for a surprise 40th birthday.

Based in Austin, TX. Open to in-house brand design and art direction roles.
