Overview

Healthcare's own visual identity — built from the ground up

Careers in Care (CiC) is the flagship healthcare campaign within Indeed's Category system, targeting nurses and other healthcare workers — one of the most supply-constrained job categories on the platform. The goal: make healthcare workers feel genuinely seen by a brand that's usually associated with desk jobs.

I designed the CiC brand identity, the event environment, and the earned media assets for both the 2023 and 2024 editions — and extended it into the "Off the Clock" event experience in 2025.

2023 — Art Direction

An original photoshoot built around a real nurse

In 2023, I art directed the Careers in Care photoshoot — building the set design concept and directing the full session to produce owned photography that could anchor the campaign across every channel. No stock imagery: just a real nurse, a considered set, and a creative brief designed to feel human and specific rather than generic "healthcare."

The resulting library became the visual backbone for social, paid display, email, and out-of-home executions throughout the year.

2024 — Evolution

"Game On" — extending the system with a new concept

In 2024, I extended the Careers in Care system with a new "Game On" set design concept and badge identity — evolving the visual language while keeping it rooted in the original system. The 2024 edition also expanded into a full magazine feature in ADA Magazine and lifecycle marketing programs that moved healthcare workers through targeted email-to-apply journeys.

2025 — Off the Clock

Event branding for healthcare workers — feels like a night out, not a conference

"Off the Clock" is the 2025 Careers in Care event — a brand experience designed for healthcare workers to connect, celebrate, and recharge. I created the full event identity: branded lanyards, step-and-repeat backdrop, tall-format event banners, partnership co-branding, and branded merchandise including water bottles.

The Off the Clock identity extended the Careers in Care visual system while carving out its own warm, celebratory personality — distinct from the day-to-day campaign work.
